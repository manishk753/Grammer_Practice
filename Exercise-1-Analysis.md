### **Sentence 12**: "The sea [hath may thousand sands]"

### **Sentence 19**: "[All roads lead to] Rome"
- **Error**: Incorrect predicate identification
- **Issue**: The bracketed part includes the predicate AND the object. "Rome" is the object, not part of the predicate.
- **Correct Form**: **Subject**: All roads | **Predicate**: [lead to Rome]
- **Should be**: "[All roads lead to Rome]" OR "All roads [lead to] Rome"

### **Sentence 21**: "The beautiful rainbow [soon faded away]"
- **Error**: Adverb placement issue
- **Issue**: "soon" is an adverb modifying the predicate but marks timing. The predicate should be [faded away], with "soon" as an adverbial modifier.
- **Correct Identification**: **Subject**: The beautiful rainbow | **Predicate**: [faded away] (with "soon" as adverbial modifier)
- **Current marking is acceptable but imprecise**

### **Sentence 22**: "No man [can serve two master]"
- **Error**: Grammar mistake - Subject-Verb agreement
- **Issue**: "master" should be "masters" (plural to match "two")
- **Correct Form**: "No man [can serve two masters]"
- **Subject-Predicate**: Correct structure ⚠️ (but contains error)

### **Sentence 23**: "A sick room [should be well aired]"
- **Error**: Awkward/archaic phrasing

### **Sentence 24**: "I shot [an arrow in the air]"
- **Error**: Incorrect predicate identification
- **Issue**: "an arrow in the air" is the direct object, not part of the predicate
- **Correct Form**: **Subject**: I | **Predicate**: [shot] (with "an arrow in the air" as the object)

### **Sentence 26**: "[A barking sound] the shepherd [hears]"

- **Current marking**: Brackets are misplaced

### **Sentence 27**: "[On the top of the hill lives a] hermit"
- **Error**: Incorrect predicate bracketing
- **Issue**: "On the top of the hill" is a prepositional phrase (adverbial modifier), not part of the predicate
- **Correct Form**: **Subject**: A hermit | **Predicate**: [lives] / [lives on the top of the hill]
- **Should be**: "On the top of the hill [lives a hermit]" OR "A hermit [lives on the top of the hill]"

## Key Takeaways:

1. **Predicate** = Verb + all words that complete the verb's meaning
2. **Don't include objects in predicates** when bracketing (Sentences 19, 24)
3. **Watch inverted sentences** - identify subject and predicate by meaning, not position (Sentences 26, 27)
4. **Adverbial modifiers** are separate from the core predicate structure
5. **Grammar rules**: Watch subject-verb agreement and spelling consistency
